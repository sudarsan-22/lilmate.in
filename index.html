Update your lil.mate

Pick whatever you have. Your tasks, habits and settings stay. If anything goes wrong, lil.mate keeps running the version it has.

…checking the newest version

Over Wi-Fi easiest

Any phone or computer, iPhone too. lil.mate downloads it by itself.

  1. lil.mate checks for new versions once a day and says "New update" on its screen.
  2. Hold lil.mate to install. (Missed it? Go to its Info screen and hold.)
  3. Or on your phone: open lilmate.local → More → Updates → Install.
Open lilmate.local

Can't open lilmate.local on Android? Use the address shown on lil.mate's Info screen, like 192.168.0.10.

USB cable

Windows, Mac, Linux or Chromebook, in Chrome or Edge. No Wi-Fi needed.

  1. Plug lil.mate into the computer with its USB-C cable.
  2. Quit the lil.mate Desk app if it's open (only one app can use the cable).
  3. Press Connect and pick USB JTAG/serial (or lil.mate) in the list.

Bluetooth

Android phones (Chrome) and computers with Bluetooth. Good for offices without Wi-Fi.

  1. Bluetooth turns on by itself when lil.mate has no Wi-Fi. If it has Wi-Fi, send /bluetooth to it (Telegram, lilmate.local or the Desk app).
  2. Press Connect and pick lil.mate.
  3. When lil.mate asks, hold it to allow the update. Keep the phone close.

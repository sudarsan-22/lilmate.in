# Desk Mini v2 - box cutting template and guide

Box outside 70 x 68 x 63 mm. Flat sheet 291 x 235 mm. Board: 1.5 mm kraft E-flute, flutes running ALONG the long fold lines.

## Files
- `DESK_MINI_BOX_TEMPLATE_FINAL.gcode.3mf` - sliced for the Bambu A1 mini: 2 plates, about 2 h 25 min and 71 g of PLA in total (0.2 mm layers, 3 mm thick).
- `stl/` - 6 panel tiles (`desk_mini-front/sideR/back/sideL/lid/base.stl`), the bow-tie key (`desk_mini-key.stl`) and 12 key copies in `stl/keys/` (10 are needed, 2 spares).
- `desk_mini_dieline_1to1_A4.pdf` - the dieline at 100% on A4 pages; tape together and cut a paper or thin-card MOCK-UP first.
- `desk_mini_template_preview.png` - how the tiles sit together.

## Assemble the template
1. Print all plates. Check one tile against the 1:1 paper dieline.
2. Lay the six tiles flat in the dieline layout (see preview). Neighbouring tiles are separated by a 1.4 mm gap, which is the score line.
3. Press two bow-tie keys into the matching pockets across each joint (10 keys: 6 on the vertical joints, 4 on the horizontal joints). Add a spot of tape under a key if it feels loose.

## Cut and score
1. Place the sheet so the long fold lines run along the flutes. Put the assembled template on it and hold it down.
2. Cut around the OUTER edge of the template with a snap-off knife (new blade, 2-3 light passes). Cut the thumb-notch circle too.
3. Score every slot and every gap between tiles with a blunt stylus (dry ballpoint or the back of the knife) pressing about 0.5 mm into the board, not through. The slots stop 6 mm short of the edges; finish those ends with a ruler where the small edge notches show the line.
4. Lift the template off. Fold along the scored lines, glue flap inside the front panel, then fold the dust flaps in before closing the lid.

## Check
- Panels should fold square; if the box is slightly tight, widen only the window of the cradle, not the template.
- If the knife has shaved an edge, reprint that tile.

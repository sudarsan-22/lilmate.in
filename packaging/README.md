# Packaging

One box design (tuck-top, 1.5 mm kraft E-flute, one die-cut sheet), made in three fitted sizes.

| Product | Inside (mm) | Outside (mm) | Flat sheet (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deck Buddy | 95 x 95 x 68 | 98 x 98 x 71 | 407 x 303 |
| Elephant | 126 x 112 x 88 | 129 x 115 x 91 | 503 x 357 |
| Desk Mini | 67 x 65 x 60 | 70 x 68 x 63 | 291 x 235 |

Each product lies face up on a cradle cut from stacked 1.5 mm layers (outline of the product +1 mm), with a 6 mm cable and card tray underneath.
Desk Mini's assembled size (47 x 45 x 38) is an estimate: measure it before cutting. Dielines are a first pass: make a paper mock-up before cutting corrugate.

# Reel 02: "A day with Minimo" — shot list

A 20–25 s reel, Apple-ad style: real hands, real desk, real light, very few words.
You film it on a phone and put the clips in `clips/`. Claude edits it: cuts, color grade, speed ramps, titles, sound, end card.

**The story in one line:** Minimo is asleep in the morning. A pat wakes it up, it keeps you company through the day, and it dozes off again at night.

---

## 1. Before you film

### The set (one desk, used all day)
- **Surface:** light wood or a cream/beige cloth. No clutter, nothing with a logo.
- **Props, three at most:** a ceramic mug, a small plant, a closed notebook. The laptop only appears in shots 5 and 6.
- **Background:** a plain wall 1–2 m behind the desk, so it falls into soft blur. Cream, beige or soft blue suits the brand.
- **Hero shot (shot 9):** an A3 sheet of cream card curved up the wall behind Minimo (a "sweep"), plus a beige paper cut-out shape behind it, like the website.

### Light (this is what makes it look premium)
- **One big soft light from the side:** a window with a thin white curtain, at 45° to the desk. Switch the ceiling lights off.
- A sheet of white paper or foam board on the other side bounces light back into the shadows.
- **Evening shots (7 and 8):** one warm desk lamp only, room dark.
- Minimo's screen is bright. **Lower the exposure** (tap to focus on Minimo, then drag the sun icon down until the white eyes no longer burn out). The eyes should look white with a soft glow, not a blown-out blob.

### Phone settings
- **4K, 24 fps** for normal shots. Pro iPhones: use **Apple Log** if available, otherwise **Cinematic mode is off** and **HDR video is off** (it grades better).
- **Slow-motion 1080p 120 fps** for the taps and pats (shots 2, 4 and 6).
- **Lock focus and exposure** before each take: long-press on Minimo until "AE/AF LOCK" shows.
- Use the 2× or 3× lens for close-ups. It flattens the background and looks expensive. Use 1× only for shot 5.
- Clean the lens. Turn on airplane mode so no notification lands mid-take.
- **Steady camera:** tripod, or the phone on a stack of books. For slow push-ins, put the phone on a folded towel and slide it slowly towards Minimo. Shoot 3–4 takes.

### Who is in it
- One person. Mostly **hands, arms and over-the-shoulder** shots; the face, if shown, only in soft focus.
- Plain clothing in cream, beige, oatmeal or white: a knit sweater is ideal. No logos, no smart watch.
- Clean, natural hands; short nails. Slow, gentle movements, about half your normal speed.

### Getting Minimo's faces on cue
You don't have to wait for moods. From lil.mate Desk (type in the "Tell lil.mate something…" box), the phone dashboard, or Telegram:

| You want | Send |
|---|---|
| Asleep | `/face sleep` (natural sleep comes after 5 min untouched) |
| Sleepy | `/face sleepy` |
| Happy | `/face happy` or a real tap on the head |
| Heart eyes | hold its head (cuddle), or `/face love` |
| Dizzy | hold its head for 4 s, or `/face dizzy` |
| A reminder pops up | `/remind 1m drink water` (wait 1 min) |
| Focused eyes | `/face focus` |
| Curious / looking around | `/face curious` |

Real touches look best, so use the commands only when a real touch is not in the shot. Turn brightness up for daylight shots: `/brightness 255`.

## 2. The shots

Film every shot **4–8 seconds long** with 2 s of stillness before and after the action. Do 3+ takes of each.
Filenames go in `clips/` exactly as written below (add `_take2`, `_take3` and so on).

| # | File | Shot | Camera | Action | Minimo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01_asleep.mov` | Morning. Minimo alone on the desk, first soft light | Low, at Minimo's eye level, 3×, slow push-in | Nothing moves. Let the light do it | Asleep (dim) |
| 2 | `02_pat_wake.mov` | **The hero moment.** A finger taps its head | Same angle, 2–3×, **slow-mo** | Hand enters from the right, one gentle tap, hand leaves | Wakes and turns happy |
| 3 | `03_smile.mov` | The person's reaction | Over the shoulder, Minimo sharp, person soft | A small smile, a chin rest on the hand | Happy, blinking |
| 4 | `04_mug.mov` | Morning ritual | Top-down (phone flat above the desk) | Mug set down beside Minimo, steam if possible | Looking at the mug |
| 5 | `05_work_wide.mov` | Working together | 1×, from behind and to the side, laptop + Minimo + person | Typing; lil.mate Desk pill visible at the top of the screen | Focused eyes (`/face focus`) |
| 6 | `06_reminder.mov` | A reminder arrives | Close on Minimo, 3× | Screen shows the alert; finger taps once to dismiss; person picks up the glass of water | Alert, then happy |
| 7 | `07_cuddle.mov` | Evening, warm lamp | Close, 2–3×, slow-mo optional | Palm rests on its head for 2 s | Heart eyes |
| 8 | `08_goodnight.mov` | Night | Wide-ish, lamp is the only light | Person closes the laptop and switches off the lamp (hand only) | Sleepy, then asleep (`/face sleepy`, then `/face sleep`) |
| 9 | `09_hero_spin.mov` | Product hero | On the cream sweep, 2×, eye level | Rotate Minimo slowly by hand or on a lazy-susan / turntable, about 90° over 6 s | Happy, blinking |
| 10 | `10_macro_eyes.mov` | Detail | As close as the phone can focus (macro mode) | Nothing; eyes blink | Neutral, then happy |

**Optional bonus shots** (use them if they turn out well):
- `11_dizzy.mov`: 4 s hold, then a dizzy face and the person laughs.
- `12_game.mov`: playing Star Catcher with the laptop arrow keys, Minimo's screen in the foreground.
- `13_two_buddies.mov`: two buddies side by side doing a team game (if you have two).

## 3. What the edit will look like

The edit is about 22 s. Words come from the website's own voice, one short line at a time, in Fredoka on the footage:

| Time | Shots | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | 1 → 10 | *(no words; the sleeping-particle "z" floats up)* |
| 3–6 s | 2 (slow-mo tap) → 3 | **Pat it.** → **It smiles back.** |
| 6–11 s | 4 → 5 → 6 | **Keeps you company.** · **Reminds you to drink water.** |
| 11–15 s | 7 (and 11 if used) | **Hold it.** → **Heart eyes.** |
| 15–18 s | 8 | **Leave it alone…** → **it naps too.** |
| 18–22 s | 9 → end card | **A lil mate for your desk.** · logo · Pre-order yours · lilmate.in |

Grade: warm cream highlights, soft contrast, slightly lifted blacks, light film grain. Same palette as the site (cream `#fff8ee`, ink `#24130f`, orange `#f7931e`).
Sound: soft piano/pad bed, a gentle "boop" on each tap, room tone. A version without audio is exported too, for adding a trending track in Instagram.
